====

[[req-dot-patterns-dotpattern]]
===== Fill `dotpattern` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/dot-patterns/dotpattern
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `dotpattern` property within the `Fill` class, defined as a `Dotpattern` class.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support filling vector geometry with a regular grid of points based on a `dotpattern` property.
====

[[req-dot-patterns-distance]]
===== Dotpattern `distance` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/dot-patterns/distance
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `distance` Dotpattern property as a Distance object including a `horizontal` and a `vertical` float.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support spacing the rendered points of a dot pattern fill horizontally by the `horizontal` distance and vertically by the `vertical` distance.
====
