[[req-arithmetic-add]]
===== Arithmetic `add` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/arithmetic/add
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining an `add` operation expression taking two numeric operands.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating an `add` operation expression as the sum of its two operands.
====

[[req-arithmetic-sub]]
===== Arithmetic `sub` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/arithmetic/sub
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `sub` operation expression taking two numeric operands.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating a `sub` operation expression as the first operand minus the second operand.
====

[[req-arithmetic-mul]]
===== Arithmetic `mul` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/arithmetic/mul
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `mul` operation expression taking two numeric operands.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating a `mul` operation expression as the product of its two operands.
====

[[req-arithmetic-div]]
===== Arithmetic `div` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/arithmetic/div
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `div` operation expression taking two numeric operands.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating a `div` operation expression as the real-valued quotient of the first operand divided by the second operand, including when both operands are integers.
====

[[req-arithmetic-int-div]]
===== Arithmetic `intDiv` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/arithmetic/int-div
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining an `intDiv` operation expression taking two numeric operands.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating an `intDiv` operation expression as the integer quotient of the first operand divided by the second operand, truncated toward zero.
====

[[req-arithmetic-mod]]
===== Arithmetic `mod` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/arithmetic/mod
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `mod` operation expression taking two numeric operands.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating a `mod` operation expression as the remainder of the first operand divided by the second operand, consistent with the `intDiv` operator (i.e., the result has the sign of the first operand).
====

[[req-arithmetic-pow]]
===== Arithmetic `pow` operator

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/arithmetic/pow
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a `pow` operation expression taking two numeric operands.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating a `pow` operation expression as the first operand raised to the power of the second operand.
====
