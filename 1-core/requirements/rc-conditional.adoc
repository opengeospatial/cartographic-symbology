[[req-conditional-condition]]
===== Conditional `condition` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/conditional/condition
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a ConditionalExpression with a `condition` property as an Expression evaluating to a boolean value.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support evaluating the `condition` of a ConditionalExpression to select whether the ConditionalExpression resolves to its `thenExp` or its `elseExp` expression.
====

[[req-conditional-then-exp]]
===== Conditional `thenExp` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/conditional/then-exp
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a ConditionalExpression with a `thenExp` property as an Expression.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support resolving a ConditionalExpression to the value of its `thenExp` expression when its `condition` evaluates to true.
====

[[req-conditional-else-exp]]
===== Conditional `elseExp` property

[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/conditional/else-exp
part:: An Encoding SHALL support defining a ConditionalExpression with an `elseExp` property as an Expression.
part:: A Renderer SHALL support resolving a ConditionalExpression to the value of its `elseExp` expression when its `condition` does not evaluate to true.
====
